# Content review

An independent review checked all 24 examples and found the type coverage, Core skill pyramids, stress derivations, consequence allocations, starting stunt/refresh bookkeeping, separate FAE approach example and optional custom-skill boundaries consistent. It also checked player agency, alternative discovery routes and the connected references.

Two editorial corrections were then applied before the final export:

- The stamp clue now proves only the sequence of writing and stamping, not authorship or intent. The Office's explicit completed-roll/recertification rule supplies the legal inference, and the clue tells the GM to verify the witness register before concluding a stay is justified.
- The shared escalation now has an explicit order: warning at 23:50, records transfer at 00:00, special collection bell at 00:10, threatened purist attack at 00:15. Matching faction, adventure and setting text use that order.

All cards and the PDF were regenerated from the corrected source. Final layout measurements found no text/footer overlap or overflow on any of the 24 PDF pages. The final PDF's page count is 24. Individual cards retain the auto-height format; they are not fixed physical index-card dimensions. These are original, GM-facing demonstrations, not official or adopted campaign material.
