The Margin Hound
Creature · Supernatural creature · supporting opposition
NPC
GM DEMO · NOT CANON
Aspects: A Paper Beast That Smells Broken Promises; Cannot Hunt a Debt Honestly Forgiven; Bound to the Office Seal.
Appearance / behavior: A folded-black-paper hound, its ribs covered in crossed-out signatures. It circles a debtor before choosing which promise to track.
Want: Consume the written record of an unfulfilled obligation. Its keeper uses that appetite to patrol the collection engine.
Mechanics: Fight +3, Notice +2, Athletics +1, Physique +1; unlisted +0. Physical [1][2][3], mental [1][2], one box per hit. Empty mild consequence 2.
Stunt proposal: Scent the Default: +2 Notice to create an advantage tracking a specific broken promise while holding its signed record. Not general lie detection.
Counterplay: An honestly forgiven debt loses its scent. A different signed broken promise can draw the hound away; a convincing false scent requires an appropriate create-advantage action. It is not obliged to attack someone who offers food.
GM truth: It guards records, not Vale personally. Its binding can be transferred by a lawful Office seal; destroying it is only one possible solution.
