The Office of Civic Time
Organization · Formal institution · registry and public utility
FACTION
GM DEMO · NOT CANON
Aspects: Every Borrowed Hour Must Be Accounted For; The Seal Outranks the Person Holding It.
Mandate / structure: Registers voluntary time loans and runs the hospital reserve. Collectors deliver warrants; auditors certify consent; a commissioner authorizes collection.
Resources / weakness: Controls the bell tower, official seals and maintenance crews. Lawful collection requires two separately timestamped witnesses to certify a completed roll with every debtor named. Adding or changing a debtor requires fresh certification by both witnesses; the altered roll was never recertified.
Faces / disagreement: Vale controls policy. Nera knows field practice. Night auditor Ansel wants a defensible audit, not a revolution, and can issue a temporary stay.
Access routes: A valid petition, a public hearing, a staff ally or a covert service entrance can reach the same records with different risks.
GM use: Run the Office as people and procedures, not a single evil mind. A lawful stay can halt collection while preserving hospital supply. No faction-scale subsystem is assumed.
