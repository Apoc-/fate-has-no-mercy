Ledgerglass
Item · Investigative tool · no separate stunt cost
ITEM
GM DEMO · NOT CANON
Description: A thumb-sized lens with a hairline of captive dawn running through its rim.
Fictional permission: Under strong light it reveals physical overwriting, lifted fibers and ink layers in a document. It does not identify the author, read minds or prove that a statement is true.
Use in Fate: Makes relevant Investigate actions possible where the alteration is too fine for the naked eye. No automatic numeric bonus; create an advantage or invoke an established aspect normally.
Complication: Its light reveals the examiner's reflection in nearby polished brass. A watcher may spot who is inspecting the roll.
Where to find it: Pell loans it against a promise to return a missing index slip. Nera has seen Vale use the same lens on his private copies.
