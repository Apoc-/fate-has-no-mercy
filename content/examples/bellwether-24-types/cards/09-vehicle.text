The Mothlight
Vehicle · Bellwind skiff · pilot-operated vehicle
ITEM
GM DEMO · NOT CANON
Aspect: Swift Between Chimes, Helpless in the Silence
Capabilities: Carries a pilot and three passengers along the tower's resonant airways. Its fabric keel can skim roofs but cannot sustain flight when the bellwind stops.
Piloting: This setting uses Core's Drive for piloted conveyances. Roll only when opposition or a meaningful hazard makes the outcome uncertain; the skiff has no independent action or skill rating.
Useful fittings: A folding retrieval hook and a canvas sound-baffle can support create-advantage actions. Neither grants automatic free invokes.
Risk / counterplay: The thirteenth bell will reverse the main airway. Land before the marked warning chime, take the sheltered maintenance channel, or secure a roofline tow.
Ownership: Lark lends it for the witness extraction. If vehicle damage becomes central, agree a vehicle-extra model before play rather than inventing HP during the chase.
