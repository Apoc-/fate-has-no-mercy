Echo of the Last Minute
Power · Original supernatural power proposal · Fate Core extra
NOTE
GM DEMO · NOT CANON
Permission / cost: Requires a chronomancy-related aspect and an attuned hourglass. Costs one stunt slot; an additional paid stunt reduces refresh by 1 if the character has used their free allocation. Not included in Ira's starting sheet.
Effect: Once per scene, spend a fate point while touching a fixed surface to observe its last minute as a faint sensory echo from that location. This grants access to the recorded event, not an automatic advantage or a guaranteed answer.
Limits: One vantage, no thoughts, no future events and no events beyond the last minute. Walls still block sight. Illusions in the original event remain illusions.
Opposition / follow-up: An active temporal ward can oppose access with Lore; use overcome only when that opposition matters. Investigating an unclear echo is a separate relevant action.
Counterplay / cost in fiction: Move the evidence out of range, obscure the scene or let the minute pass. The hourglass glows while used, so observers can recognize the attempt. Table approval required.
