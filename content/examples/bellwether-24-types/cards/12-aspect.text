I Owe Tomorrow to Someone I Haven't Met
Aspect · Character trouble · two-edged authoring example
NOTE
GM DEMO · NOT CANON
Meaning: A binding promise saved you once. Its unknown holder can someday demand repayment, but that promise also marks you as someone trusted by an unusual future ally.
Useful applications: Invoke when that obligation gives you credibility with time-brokers, or when you draw on preparations made to honor the promise. Explain relevance and pay or use a valid free invoke normally.
Complication applications: A compel could bring a repayment demand during a rescue, or reveal that a useful official is the debt holder whose interests oppose yours. Negotiate the complication; it does not force a player action or mean a roll must fail.
Not a power: The aspect alone does not grant prophecy, time travel or a standing +2. Establish supernatural permissions separately.
Connected use: This is Ira Venn's trouble. Decide who holds the debt through play or with the player, not by secretly rewriting established history.
