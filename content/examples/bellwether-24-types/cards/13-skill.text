Chronometry
Skill · Optional custom skill · NOT added to the Core examples
NOTE
GM DEMO · NOT CANON
Design purpose: For a campaign where temporal engineering deserves its own spotlight, split that specialty from Lore. Agree the revised skill list and rebalance/rebuild allocations together; do not add free ratings on top of existing skills.
Overcome: Calibrate a collection engine, diagnose a temporal seal, or stabilize an out-of-phase instrument when failure matters.
Create an advantage: Identify a skipped cycle, establish a safe timing window or expose a falsified timestamp through temporal analysis.
Attack / defend: No normal attacks. Defend against attempts to sabotage a timing process you are actively controlling, not against bullets or every form of magic.
Boundary: Investigate still examines mundane evidence, Crafts repairs ordinary machinery, and Lore covers other knowledge. If the specialty rarely matters, keep it in Lore and differentiate experts with aspects/stunts instead.
