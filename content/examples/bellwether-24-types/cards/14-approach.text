Careful
Approach · Separate Fate Accelerated example · not a Core bonus
NOTE
GM DEMO · NOT CANON
What it describes: How a character acts: methodical checks, preparation and avoiding unnecessary risk. It is not a domain skill or an extra rating added to Lore.
Example action: A chronologist tries to align a witness cylinder without erasing it. A Careful approach means inspecting each detent, marking the original position and testing the mechanism slowly.
Sample rating: Careful +2 in a separate FAE character build: roll 4dF +2 when that approach genuinely fits. This is the approach rating, not a free invoke or a bonus on the Core sheets.
Trade-off in fiction: Taking time may expose the character to the approaching inspection. Careful does not automatically prevent failure or add a permanent penalty; consequences follow the situation.
Other routes: A Quick attempt could beat the inspector to the chamber; a Clever attempt could reconstruct alignment from the sound. Choose the actual method, not merely the highest number.
