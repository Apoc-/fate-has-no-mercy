Hear the Missing Beat
Stunt · Original Fate Core stunt proposal · included on Ira's sheet
NOTE
GM DEMO · NOT CANON
Rule text: Gain +2 to Notice when creating an advantage to identify disrupted timing in a repetitive mechanism after listening to one complete cycle.
Scope: One skill: Notice. One action: create an advantage. One circumstance: a complete observed cycle of a repetitive mechanism. No bonus to attacks, general detection, testimony or every temporal problem.
Example: Listen to the collection engine and identify a double-counted interval. A successful action might establish A Missing Beat Between Signatures, with the normal outcome's free invoke allocation.
Limits / cost: Listening takes the fictional time the cycle requires. Costs one stunt allocation; no separate fate-point activation cost. This is one of Ira's three free starting stunts, not a fourth.
Review: Approve the trigger and relevance with the table. The fiction must actually contain disrupted timing; the stunt is not permission to create evidence retroactively.
