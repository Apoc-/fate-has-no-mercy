Who Signed for the Unborn Hour?
Mystery · GM mystery framework · redundant discovery routes
NOTE
GM DEMO · NOT CANON
Question / truth: Who added unsigned citizens to tomorrow's collection roll? Vale inserted the names after hours to conceal the hospital reserve's shortfall, then countersigned his own forgery.
Essential conclusion: The roll lacks valid consent and can be stayed without immediately stopping the hospital reserve.
Independent routes: Archive: debtor names were added after witness certification; the posted Office rule requires fresh witnesses. Nera: her hidden receipt connects the changes to the countersigner, and she can attest no recertification occurred. Office: auditor Ansel can verify the missing fresh witness records. These are three routes, not three mandatory checks.
Fair complication: The Unwritten's blank forms resemble the altered paper, but their watermark identifies a different batch. Reveal that distinguishing evidence; do not treat an innocent faction's style as proof.
Why it matters: Proof opens a lawful stay, leverage over Vale, or a public hearing. It does not decide which response the PCs must choose.
Run it: Provide obvious evidence when characters can reasonably see it. Rolls can determine speed, detail or risk; no single failed check should erase every path to the required conclusion.
