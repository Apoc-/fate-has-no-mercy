The Night Without Tomorrow
Adventure · One-session adventure · three connected nodes
NOTE
GM DEMO · NOT CANON
Entry: Nera brings the party a roll bearing their names and an unsigned collection warrant. At the thirteenth bell the engine will act on it unless someone intervenes.
Node 1 · Archive: Compare receipt times, meet Pell and locate the consent rule. Leads point to Nera's stamp at the auction and Ansel's authority at the Office.
Node 2 · Auction: Secure the witness stamp, a broker's delivery note or a buyer willing to testify. Each can connect Vale to the engine without requiring a specific theft or purchase.
Node 3 · Engine / Office: Choose a covert intervention, a lawful stay or a public confrontation. The hound guards one route; the maintenance channel and audit route remain alternatives.
If unopposed / timeline: 23:50 warning chime; 00:00 records transfer; 00:10 special thirteenth bell collects the forged debt; 00:15 the purist cell attempts to burn the Archive index. Forewarning gives the PCs room to change any event.
Possible resolutions: Suspend the illegal roll and seek informed voluntary replacement loans; isolate the hospital reserve before disabling collection; expose Vale and demand an emergency civic vote. Reward unexpected workable solutions, not only these examples.
Aftermath: Who now controls the reserve? Which witnesses trust the PCs? End on changed relationships and a remaining issue; do not force a predetermined ending.
