Bellwether
Setting · Urban clockpunk fantasy setting pitch
NOTE
GM DEMO · NOT CANON
Premise: A cliffside city powers its hospitals, lifts and night markets with voluntarily borrowed hours. Signed time-debts are tangible records, and their collection changes what people can do with their coming days.
Current issue: The City Runs on Borrowed Tomorrows: useful public infrastructure depends on debts whose consent is no longer trustworthy.
Impending issue / timeline: The Thirteenth Bell Will Collect from the Unwilling. The warning sounds at 23:50, records transfer at midnight, the special thirteenth collection bell sounds at 00:10, and the purist cell plans its attack for 00:15. These are threatened events the PCs can change.
Faces / forces: Nera Quill offers testimony; Vale offers stability at a human cost; the Unwritten offers freedom with an internal argument over evidence and destruction.
Places / technology: The Archive hangs below the tower; bellwind skiffs cross the upper lanes; the hospital reserve is physically separable from the collection engine. Magic records events but is not automatic mind-reading or prophecy.
PC invitation: Who once lent you an hour? Whose name would you risk your future to protect? Pick an answer together and connect it to another PC.
Tone / boundaries: Street-level wonder, civic intrigue and difficult bargains, not mandatory grimdark. Agree comfort with debt, lost time and institutional coercion before play. All fiction here is a new proposal.
