A Season of Stolen Tomorrows
Campaign · Campaign framework · open consequences, not a fixed plot
NOTE
GM DEMO · NOT CANON
Campaign question: Can Bellwether keep its life-saving wonders without allowing anyone to own another person's future?
Arc 1 · Consent: Begin with The Night Without Tomorrow. Follow the forged roll and decide what proof, people and infrastructure to preserve.
Arc 2 · Dependence: Explore who profits from lawful loans as well as fraud. Neighboring districts offer rival solutions; the Unwritten and Office can split, reform or cooperate according to play.
Arc 3 · Ownership: Confront the founding compact behind the tower. The future debt holder in Ira's trouble can emerge through player agreement; their identity and solution are not pre-scripted.
Moving interests: Vale seeks a legacy or escape; the rescue wing protects people; the purists target records; hospital staff need a functioning reserve. Advance plans only when the fiction and PCs' choices warrant it.
Milestones: Use Core milestones at appropriate fiction-driven points: minor opportunities to adjust a character, significant milestones for scenario/arc development, major milestones for major changes. Apply the actual advancement rules, not automatic rewards per session.
Ending space: A reformed utility, decentralized time commons, a city that abandons the tower, or a costly new compact are all possible. Let decisions establish the outcome.
