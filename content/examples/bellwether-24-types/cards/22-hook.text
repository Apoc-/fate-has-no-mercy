Your Name, Tomorrow's Roll
Hook · Immediate actionable opening
NOTE
GM DEMO · NOT CANON
Say at the table: A woman in a red collector's coat puts a warrant on your table. Your name is beneath tomorrow's date. The consent signature is not yours. 'I delivered one of these yesterday,' she says. 'I won't deliver this one.'
Offer / urgency: Nera offers the duplicate and a way into the auction gallery if the PCs protect her testimony. The warning chime sounds before midnight; the collection is later, at the thirteenth bell.
Choices: Verify the warrant, protect Nera, approach the Office, investigate the debtor named beside you, or refuse and seek another way to keep your name off the roll.
If declined: Nera approaches auditor Ansel. Her attempt can change the situation without forcing the PCs to accept the job or punishing refusal with unavoidable failure.
GM placement: Connect a borrowed hour or named debtor to a PC only with player agreement. The forged signature is a problem to investigate, not permission to rewrite a character's agreed past.
