The Stamp Beneath the Signature
Clue · GM clue · physical fact, discovery and implication
NOTE
GM DEMO · NOT CANON
Observable fact: On the duplicate roll, the debtor-name ink crosses the witness stamp's dried ridge without being indented by it: the names were written after the stamp was applied. This establishes sequence, not authorship or intent.
Ways to discover: Compare the original and carbon copy at the Archive; inspect the layering with Ledgerglass; or ask Pell to demonstrate how the Office press leaves its ridge. These are alternative channels for one clue.
Inference / next action: Office rules require witnesses to certify the completed named roll and demand fresh certification for later name changes. The layering therefore raises a specific recertification defect; check the witness register or ask Ansel. If no fresh certification exists, request a stay. Question the countersigner or obtain the earlier impression to investigate responsibility.
What it does NOT prove: It does not by itself identify who changed the roll. Nera's receipt and Vale's countersignature independently connect the commissioner.
Roll policy: If the ridge is plainly demonstrated, give the fact. A relevant roll may reveal it more quickly or create an advantage for the hearing, not decide whether the entire mystery remains solvable.
