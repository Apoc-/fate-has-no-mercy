What Interrupts the Next Bell?
Random table · 1d6 situation spark · original table
NOTE
GM DEMO · NOT CANON
1 · Unexpected ally: An off-duty collector recognizes Nera and offers a lawful route through a closed door, but needs a witness for their own protection.
2 · Evidence changes hands: The witness cylinder's buyer wants to resell it before leaving. Negotiate, follow or expose the sale; ownership is not proof of guilt.
3 · A route opens: A stalled service lift exposes a crawlway to the Archive. The keeper will hold it for a practical favor, not an inevitable bribe.
4 · Danger announces itself: A false beat reverses the upper bellwind. The warning gives skiff passengers time to land, tether up or choose the sheltered route.
5 · Competing priority: Hospital staff ask for help separating the reserve before any shutdown. They can explain the relevant controls; this need not erase another solution.
6 · Faction disagreement: The Unwritten's purist cell arrives with oil; its rescue wing asks the PCs to preserve the witness records. Decide which argument or alternative to offer.
Use: Roll one ordinary d6 when an interruption would help, or choose. Adapt to established facts; do not force a duplicate ally, contradict a secured clue or impose a player decision. Entries create opportunities, pressure and changes, not only punishment.
